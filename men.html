Men's Ministry

Raising men of integrity, strength, and spiritual depth — who lead with courage at home, in the workplace, and in the world.

Our Purpose

Iron sharpens iron — men growing stronger together

The Men's Ministry is a brotherhood committed to helping men become who God created them to be. In a world that sends confusing messages about manhood, we anchor ourselves to the Word of God and the example of Jesus Christ — the ultimate model of strength and servanthood.

We tackle the real issues men face: leadership, integrity, purpose, finances, relationships, and faith. No pretense. No performance. Just men being real with each other and with God.

"Be on your guard; stand firm in the faith; be courageous; be strong. Do everything in love."

1 Corinthians 16:13–14
