Children's Church

Planting seeds of faith in the hearts of our littlest ones — because roots grown in childhood last a lifetime.

Our Mission

Teaching children that God loves them — and making it unforgettable

Children's Church is a joyful, age-appropriate environment for children from birth through age 12. Our dedicated team of trained volunteers and teachers create engaging, Bible-based lessons that connect with children at every stage of development.

We partner closely with parents, believing that what begins at church should continue at home. Our goal is to make God's Word so accessible and exciting that children carry it with them everywhere they go.

"Start children off on the way they should go, and even when they are old they will not turn from it."

Proverbs 22:6
