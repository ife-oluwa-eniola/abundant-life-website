Women's Fellowship

A sisterhood of faith where women are seen, known, supported, and celebrated — in every season of life.

Our Heart

Women of God, growing together in grace and purpose

The Women's Fellowship exists to create meaningful community among the women of our church. We believe that women are uniquely gifted by God and that when they come together in faith, extraordinary things happen — marriages are restored, callings are discovered, and lives are changed.

Whether you are single, married, a mother, a career woman, or a grandmother — you belong here. We study the Word, pray together, serve the community, and journey through life side by side.
