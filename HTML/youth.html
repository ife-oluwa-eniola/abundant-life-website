Youth Ministry

Raising a generation of bold, faith-filled young people who know who they are in Christ — and are unafraid to show it.

Our Vision

Where young lives are transformed by the power of God

Our Youth Ministry is a vibrant community for teenagers aged 13–25, designed to be a safe space where young people can ask hard questions, build deep friendships, and encounter the living God. We believe the next generation is not the church of tomorrow — they are the church of today.

Through dynamic worship, relevant Bible teaching, mentorship, and community service, we equip young people to live out their faith boldly in school, at home, and in their world.

"Don't let anyone look down on you because you are young, but set an example for the believers in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith and in purity."

1 Timothy 4:12
